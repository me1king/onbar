# Online Bar

Online Bar is an online store for drinks, snacks, and gifts in Kenya. Alcohol is for adults aged 18 and over. Online Bar is a delivery service, not a walk-in bar.

- Machakos County: Syokimau.
- Kajiado County: Kitengela, Rongai (Ongata Rongai), Ngong.
- Kiambu County: Kikuyu, Thika Road (Thika).
- Mombasa County: Nyali, Mombasa Island.
- Kisumu County: Kisumu CBD, Milimani.
- Uasin Gishu County: Eldoret CBD.
- Nakuru County: Nakuru CBD.
- Trans Nzoia County: Kitale CBD.

Eligible local Nairobi deliveries are estimated at 10–30 minutes from order placement, depending on the exact address and current delivery availability. This estimate does not apply to every listed location. Checkout confirms coverage, delivery option, fees, and the current ETA; upcountry shipping uses separate courier timelines. Product availability and fees can change. Online Bar is a delivery service, not a walk-in bar.

Alcohol is only for adults aged 18 and over. Valid identification may be requested. Offer alcohol-free drinks and water at events, and never drink and drive.
